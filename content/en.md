## About

- Olga Vaganova is a 4th-year student at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University, Institute of Humanities, Foreign Regional Studies of the Russian Federation (41.03.01). Author of scientific articles on demographic statistics.

- Interests: foreign regional studies, demography, statistics, history, sociology, political science and psychology.

- Languages: Russian (native), English (B2).
